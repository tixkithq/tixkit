A brand controls customer-facing identity within an organization. Treat domain verification, sender identity, and visual branding as separate readiness signals; changing a logo does not verify a domain or messaging provider.

## Update visual identity

Open **Settings → Branding** for the selected brand. Use organization-approved names, colors, and public assets. Preview light and dark contexts where the dashboard provides them, then save and reload.

Use assets that contain no customer data, credentials, or internal environment details. Keep critical instructions in text; a logo or screenshot must not be the only way to understand a flow.

## Verify the public result

Open a published test event or checkout preview in a private browser window. Confirm the expected brand, not merely the admin preview. Cached public content may require a new revision or refresh according to the current editor workflow.

## Resolve common blockers

- A missing public logo usually means the asset was not saved, the wrong brand is selected, or the public revision has not incorporated the change.
- A custom domain is not ready until its verification status is authoritative; DNS edits alone are not completion.
- Email sender readiness is provider-specific and separate from brand display settings.
