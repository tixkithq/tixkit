An item is sellable only when its status, price, capacity, inventory pool, and sales window permit a purchase. Public availability is authoritative; an item appearing in the admin editor is not sufficient.

## Create a ticket or product

Open the event's tickets/products view. Define a customer-facing name, price and currency, capacity or inventory source, and sales window. Save and reload before creating dependent rules.

Use test values in non-production environments. Do not use an artificial zero price to bypass payment-path validation unless a free item is the intended product behavior.

## Verify availability

Open the event preview and confirm the item is visible with the expected price and remaining availability. If it is absent, check:

- draft or inactive status;
- zero or exhausted inventory;
- sales window not yet open or already closed;
- inventory pool or occurrence mismatch;
- access rules, promotion requirements, or public revision state.

## Avoid oversell assumptions

Checkout uses holds and durable confirmation behavior. Do not infer capacity from the count displayed in one browser or manually change database rows. Use supported admin/API operations and verify the resulting public availability.
